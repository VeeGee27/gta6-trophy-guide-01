Read this before you start — one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter. Whether that meditation is actually required for the trophy is still unconfirmed — PowerPyx did it and earned the trophy, so do it to be safe. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they should switch off trophies and saving.
