Read this before you start — one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they should switch off trophies and saving.

Roadmap to the Platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story (Chapters 1–5). Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and an untouched save before the final mission. Earns the 12 unmissable story trophies — the 4 chapter trophies, 7 mission trophies and the final-mission Gold.
  2. Stage 2 — Side stories. Finish the six stranger finales — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — plus 8 stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks. Do the time-sensitive Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 the moment it appears.
  3. Stage 3 — Partner and robbery tools. After Breakfast Run (Chapter 2) you can switch partners freely. Get the Snap Gun from Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs (Chapter 3) for Sneakthief, then open bank vaults with That’s Where The Money Is (late Chapter 4) for Maximum Withdrawal and Hot Together.
  4. Stage 4 — Activities and the long grind. Shooting Range golds (42), Top Fun drone races (14 locations), 20 race wins, fishing, arcade, all 18 meditation spots, 50 Challenges, 250 stunts — then push on to 300 for the 100% checklist.
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles and money. 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas, 8 garages, $250,000 of Marked Bills laundered, $100,000 spent.
  6. Stage 6 — Cleanup and 100%. After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and finish the 35-task checklist (300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, all 8 vaults, all 39 illegal operations). That earns A Long Road — and then the Platinum.
